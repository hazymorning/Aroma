# Changelog

## 0.4.0 – 2026-11-23

**New**
- Widgets for today's meals and your shopping list, in every size. Tick items off right on the home screen.
- Recipes in your measures: as written, metric or US. Oven temperatures and pan sizes in the steps change too.

**Better**
- A calmer look that is the same on every screen.

**Fixed**
- Instagram posts import again, also when Instagram hides them from the app.

## 0.3.0 – 2026-11-23

**New**
- Posts from Instagram, TikTok and Facebook now arrive as one finished recipe. You can watch each step, and a notification tells you when it's ready.
- Cookbooks, the timeline and quick filters for each meal in the Recipes tab.
- Long-press a recipe or the app icon for what you do most.
- Put the whole week on your shopping list, and move planned meals.

**Better**
- Home suggests what fits the time of day, and the ingredients can show under every cooking step.
- A new app icon, and updates install right from the settings.

**Fixed**
- Lists refresh by themselves, and signing out works without a connection.

## 0.2.0 – 2026-11-23

**New**
- A calm new look, with hand-drawn icons and a floating navigation bar.
- Plan a recipe or put it on your shopping list right below its title.
- After cooking, Home asks how it tasted.

**Better**
- Imports from Instagram and other social media are much more reliable, even when the connection drops.
- Scrolling is smoother, photos appear faster, and recipes saved offline stay up to date.

**Fixed**
- Signing out also removes saved photos and timers, and shared links no longer carry tracking.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-11-22

The first version to try out.
- Browse, search and filter your recipes, and open your cookbooks.
- Scale servings, tick off ingredients and cook with large text and timers.
- Plan your week and shop with lists that also work offline.
- Import recipes from a link, from shared text or with Mealie's AI, or write your own.
