# Changelog

## 0.8.0 – 2026-11-25

**New**
- A new logo, and a round app icon on a clear background; the settings end with a quiet version of the logo.
- Tap an ingredient where a step names it to tick it off, as in the list.
- Home greets you with a kitchen nickname that changes every day.
- Choose in the settings what Home shows; the shopping list is no longer on it unless you add it.
- Choose whether cooking mode shows the next step.

**Better**
- The meal plan is set on a clean grid: the days line up on the margin, every row is as tall as a picture, today says "Today" in sage, and an empty day has a slot to plan a dish in. Past days no longer offer to plan one.
- "Back to this week" has a line of its own under the week.

**Fixed**
- Search on Home now lands right in the search field.
- The recipes to plan for a day stand on the page margin and apart again, and "Deselect all" says what it does.

## 0.7.0 – 2026-11-24

**New**
- A new look, like a well-set cookbook: paper, ink and sage, a book face for recipes and calm, even spacing on every screen, in light and dark.
- Screens change with a quick, quiet fade.
- The meal plan shows the calendar week from Monday to Sunday: swipe to turn the week, and put the week's ingredients on your list at its end.

**Better**
- The recipe page reads like a cookbook: the times at a glance, amounts in their own column, and everything to manage it in the ⋯ menu.
- Home leads with today's meal, the week at a glance and ideas, each recipe once; save recipes with a bookmark ribbon instead of a heart.
- Large text and TalkBack work everywhere: nothing is cut off, and every button says what it does.

**Fixed**
- Long names break at sensible points, and short words are no longer hyphenated.
- Nothing runs under the status bar any more, and times like "PT1H20M" or "10–15 min" show as they should.

## 0.6.0 – 2026-11-24

**New**
- Say what kind of dish a recipe is, and pick or create categories and tags, in the editor or right on the recipe page.
- Home inspires: ideas for the time of day, dishes ready in 30 minutes, new and favourite recipes.

**Better**
- The Recipes tab helps you find something to cook: filter by meal, quick dishes or favourites, and add a recipe with a clear button.
- Pull down to refresh on every page, a calm tab bar across the bottom, and plan and shop stand out on a recipe.
- Amounts show in every step, where the recipe first names an ingredient.

**Fixed**
- Tap your star again to take a rating back.

## 0.5.0 – 2026-11-24

**New**
- Amounts in the steps: turn it on in the settings to read "75 g couscous" right in the step.
- The meal plan widget leads with the photo: just the photo at 1×1, the photo behind the name at 2×1 and 2×2.

**Better**
- Offline, your saved recipes show at once, and Aroma reconnects by itself when the server is back.
- Plan and shop are two large buttons, and the greeting stays on one line where it fits.
- In cooking mode, a swipe on the ingredient panel turns the step too.

## 0.4.2 – 2026-11-23

**New**
- Rework a recipe with AI: Mealie's AI puts its ingredients and steps in order.
- Share photos from other apps to Aroma to make a recipe from them.

**Better**
- Adding a recipe is calmer, and with AI on, Instagram posts go through Mealie's AI too.
- In cooking mode, the ingredients wait on a steady panel at the bottom.
- Smoother motion everywhere, the cook button at the bottom right, and your initial instead of your photo.

**Fixed**
- Long recipe names stay whole.

## 0.4.1 – 2026-11-23

**New**
- Import a recipe from photos, read by Mealie's AI.
- See which ingredients each step uses, in cooking mode and on the recipe page.

**Better**
- Instagram posts import in seconds. Aroma reads them itself and asks the AI only for posts in running text or to translate.

**Fixed**
- Emoji stay out of recipes, and amounts behind them scale again.

## 0.4.0 – 2026-11-23

**New**
- Widgets for today's meals and your shopping list, in every size. Tick items off right on the home screen.
- Recipes in your measures: as written, metric or US. Oven temperatures and pan sizes in the steps change too.

**Better**
- A calmer look that is the same on every screen.

**Fixed**
- Instagram posts import again, also when Instagram hides them from the app.

## 0.3.0 – 2026-11-23

**New**
- Posts from Instagram, TikTok and Facebook now arrive as one finished recipe. You can watch each step, and a notification tells you when it's ready.
- Cookbooks, the timeline and quick filters for each meal in the Recipes tab.
- Long-press a recipe or the app icon for what you do most.
- Put the whole week on your shopping list, and move planned meals.

**Better**
- Home suggests what fits the time of day, and the ingredients can show under every cooking step.
- A new app icon, and updates install right from the settings.

**Fixed**
- Lists refresh by themselves, and signing out works without a connection.

## 0.2.0 – 2026-11-23

**New**
- A calm new look, with hand-drawn icons and a floating navigation bar.
- Plan a recipe or put it on your shopping list right below its title.
- After cooking, Home asks how it tasted.

**Better**
- Imports from Instagram and other social media are much more reliable, even when the connection drops.
- Scrolling is smoother, photos appear faster, and recipes saved offline stay up to date.

**Fixed**
- Signing out also removes saved photos and timers, and shared links no longer carry tracking.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-11-22

The first version to try out.
- Browse, search and filter your recipes, and open your cookbooks.
- Scale servings, tick off ingredients and cook with large text and timers.
- Plan your week and shop with lists that also work offline.
- Import recipes from a link, from shared text or with Mealie's AI, or write your own.
